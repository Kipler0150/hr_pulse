# HR Pulse terms of use

## Internal service

HR Pulse is an internal workspace for organizations that provision team members to run basic payroll, attendance, and employee self service workflows.

## Authorized access

Use is limited to the account, organization, and role assigned to you. Keep access credentials private and use only the records and actions needed for your work. Administrators are responsible for provisioning and removing access promptly.

## Records and decisions

Payroll, attendance, payslip, and audit records may be retained to support organization operations and controlled review. Privacy settings govern optional product analytics; they do not disable security monitoring, audit history, or essential error reporting.

## Changes and availability

The service and these terms may change as the product evolves. Scheduled maintenance, provider failures, or configuration problems may temporarily limit access. The application will show safe guidance when a workflow cannot complete.

## Policy boundary

These terms are an internal, jurisdiction-neutral product policy. They do not make a legal or regulatory compliance claim. Organizations should complete appropriate legal review before using HR Pulse with real employee production data.
