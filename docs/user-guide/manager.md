# Manager guide

The manager role is for reviewing work recorded by your direct reports. You can review organization attendance and act on timecards and time off requests within your assigned review scope. Payroll setup, organization access, operations, and privacy administration belong to administrators.

## Sign in and choose the organization

1. Sign in with your provisioned work email and password.
2. Choose the organization you manage when more than one is available.
3. Check the organization name in the top bar before approving or declining anything.

Your manager access depends on an active profile, active membership, and an active employee record when the workflow needs a linked manager identity.

## Review attendance

Use **Attendance** to inspect the organization workday.

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Choose **Attendance date**.
3. Leave the field empty to use the current organization date, or enter another past or current date.
4. Choose **Review date**.
5. Review employee names, check in times, clock out times, duration, open sessions, and long interval warnings.
6. Use **Next 50 records** for the next page when the day has more records.

Attendance dates and displayed times follow the organization time zone. Future dates are not available. Approved leave markers are shown when leave data is available.

The review page is for observation. Managers do not edit attendance from that page. If an interval needs correction, ask an administrator to use the timecard correction workflow.

## Review timecards

Timecards are available when the overtime and timecards feature is enabled.

1. Open **Timecards** or **Timecard review**.
2. Review the queue for your direct reports.
3. Open a timecard and check the period, attendance evidence, regular time, overtime, pay setting snapshot, and calculation results.
4. Choose **Approve timecard** when the frozen evidence is correct.
5. Choose **Return for changes** when the employee needs to review or correct something.
6. Add a clear return note that tells the employee what to check.

Approval freezes the timecard and makes its approved earning snapshot available to payroll. A returned timecard can be resubmitted by the employee after the issue is resolved.

Do not approve a card with open attendance, missing policy or pay, stale evidence, or configuration drift. Those conditions indicate that the card needs correction or a fresh preparation.

## Review time off

Use **Time off review** to act on requests in your review scope.

1. Open **Time off review**.
2. Filter by status, date range, or employee ID when needed.
3. Open a request and read the dates, type, employee reason, and current workflow history.
4. Choose **Approve** or **Decline**.
5. Add a decision note when declining. The note must contain 1 to 500 plain text characters.
6. Confirm the updated status in the request detail.

Requests are shown in pages of up to 50. Use the next page control when more requests are available. The review page only exposes requests allowed by your organization and manager scope.

An approved request creates a leave marker that can appear with attendance. A declined request remains part of the workflow history. Do not use a new request to work around an overlapping or stale request; refresh the current request first.

## Understand access limits

Managers cannot:

* Configure payroll schedules.
* Add or change organization memberships.
* Run or confirm payroll.
* View administrator operations.
* Approve privacy deletion requests or place legal holds.

If you need one of those actions, contact an administrator. If a feature is not visible, it may also be disabled for the current environment.

## Good review practice

Review the employee’s evidence and the current version shown on the page before acting. If another person changes the record first, HR Pulse may reject the action as stale. Refresh the page and review the new state.

Use notes to explain the decision, not to include passwords, payment details, or unrelated personal information.
