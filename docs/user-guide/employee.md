# Employee guide

The employee role is for people who record their own work, submit their own time, request time off, and review the records made available to them.

## Sign in and open your workspace

1. Open HR Pulse and select **Sign in**.
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. If HR Pulse lists more than one organization, select the correct workspace and choose **Continue**.
4. Confirm the organization name in the top bar before recording work or submitting a request.

If you see **Pending access**, your profile may be inactive, your membership may be inactive, or your employee record may not be linked. Ask an administrator to check your access.

## Record attendance

Use **Attendance** to record work intervals.

1. Open **Attendance**.
2. Check the current state. The page shows whether you are checked in or checked out.
3. Choose **Check in** to start one work interval.
4. Choose **Clock out** when that work interval ends.
5. Review **Today’s intervals** after each action.

HR Pulse records the time using the database clock and displays the result in your organization’s time zone. You can have only one open interval at a time. Reloading the page shows the committed state.

Attendance history is organized by the organization calendar day. A long interval warning means the system found an unusually long open or completed interval. An approved leave marker means an approved time off request overlaps that day.

If the page says that leave data is temporarily unavailable, your attendance record is still current, but leave markers and conflict warnings are waiting for the leave data to load.

## Prepare and submit a timecard

Timecards are available when the overtime and timecards feature is enabled.

1. Open **Timecards**.
2. Choose **Review latest closed period**.
3. Review the period dates, attendance evidence, regular time, overtime, and payable amount.
4. If the period is correct, choose **Submit timecard**.
5. If the period has zero hours, confirm the zero hours checkbox before submitting.
6. If a reviewer returns the timecard, read the return note, correct the underlying attendance or setup issue when possible, and choose **Resubmit timecard**.

Submission freezes the evidence until a reviewer approves or returns it. An approved timecard is also frozen. Only approved timecards are available for payroll when the overtime feature is enabled.

If HR Pulse reports missing policy, missing pay, invalid attendance, an open interval, or a missing reviewer, ask a manager or administrator to correct the setup. Do not create a second timecard for the same period.

## Request time off

Use **Time off** to send a request to your manager or the organization reviewer.

1. Open **Time off**.
2. Enter a start date and end date.
3. Choose **Paid**, **Unpaid**, **Sick**, or **Other**.
4. Add an optional plain text reason of up to 500 characters.
5. Choose **Submit request**.
6. Open the request to see its current status and workflow history.

Requests use whole organization calendar dates. The start date must be today or later, the range can contain no more than 366 calendar days, and the dates must fall within your active employment period. Overlapping active requests are not accepted.

You can choose **Cancel request** while the request is still in a cancellable state. Once a request reaches a terminal decision, the available actions change.

When a manager or administrator makes a decision, the request detail shows the status and any decision note that is intended for you. Approved time off also appears as a marker on the relevant attendance review.

## Use self service

Self service is available only when you have an active employee record linked to your profile and the feature is enabled.

From **My self service** you can:

* Review whether your profile is complete.
* Edit your preferred name and phone number.
* Review approved timecards and their stored evidence.
* Review generated payslips.
* Reveal payslip values on the detail page when the page permits it.
* Download an authorized payslip using a short lived secure link.

Use international phone format, a plus sign followed by 7 to 15 digits. Leave the preferred name blank when you want HR Pulse to use your legal name.

The self service home page does not show pay amounts. Payslip access is limited to your own employee record in the selected organization. If a payslip download fails, refresh and try again. Do not share a signed download link.

## Manage privacy choices

When privacy controls are available, open **Privacy** to:

* Read the current privacy notice and terms.
* Turn optional product analytics on or off.
* Submit a deletion request for eligible privacy controlled data.
* Withdraw an open deletion request.
* Review your request history.

Product analytics is off until you explicitly choose to allow it. Security monitoring, audit history, and essential error reporting continue regardless of the analytics choice.

The privacy deletion feature does not remove payroll, payslip, audit history, or your employee account. If you need a correction to an HR record, contact your manager or administrator.

## If something is missing

Ask your administrator to confirm that:

* Your profile is active.
* You have an active membership in the selected organization.
* Your employee record is active and linked to your profile.
* The required feature is enabled.
* A manager or reviewer is assigned when the workflow needs one.
